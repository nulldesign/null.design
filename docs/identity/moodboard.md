# Null Design — mood board (secondary)

A textual board: references, what to take from each, and what to refuse. Images are to be collected by a person, with rights checked, before any appear on the site.

## References and what we take

| Source | Take | Leave |
|---|---|---|
| International / Swiss typography (Müller-Brockmann, Gerstner, Ruder) | grid, flush-left ragged-right, one family, hairline rules, numbered sections, asymmetry | poster scale, red-and-black cliché |
| Bell Labs technical reports and memoranda | title blocks, abstract-first, numbered figures, plain declarative captions | letterhead nostalgia |
| Unix manual pages and RFCs | NAME / SYNOPSIS / DESCRIPTION structure, monospace metadata, fixed vocabulary | fake terminal chrome |
| Early computing manuals (DEC, IBM, Xerox PARC) | exploded diagrams with callouts, specification tables, worked examples | pixel fonts, CRT glow |
| Industrial design catalogues (Braun, Olivetti, Herman Miller) | product photographed plainly on paper, dimensions as content | lifestyle staging |
| Scientific journals | figure + caption as a unit, method notes, error bars, provenance lines | two-column PDF density |
| Architectural documentation | title blocks, revision tables, north arrows — metadata as design | rendering gloss |
| Technical schematics and lab instrumentation | thin lines, labels, calibration marks, restraint | sci-fi HUD decoration |

## Refuse

purple AI gradients · glowing neural networks · robots · "futuristic" dashboards · sparkles · stock technology photography · fake terminal decoration · testimonial carousels · logo walls

## Sources of visual interest (in order of preference)

1. typography and the grid;
2. diagrams drawn for the page (SVG, hairlines, labels);
3. real screenshots with date and version in the caption;
4. CAD and fabrication drawings;
5. photographs of physical prototypes on plain paper;
6. data tables and small multiples with method notes;
7. code excerpts that a reader can run;
8. technical metadata — run IDs, registry stamps, commit hashes.

## Motion

None by default. Where an interaction needs feedback, ≤150 ms, and disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Image policy

Every image on the site is either produced by the studio or licensed with attribution in the caption. No generated imagery is passed off as a photograph. Placeholders are visible (`[ ]`) until a real artifact exists.
